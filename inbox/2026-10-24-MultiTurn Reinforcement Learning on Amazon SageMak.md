# Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning on Amazon SageMaker AI: A hands-on guide
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/multi-turn-reinforcement-learning-on-amazon-sagemaker-ai-a-hands-on-guide/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/multi-turn-reinforcement-learning-on-amazon-sagemaker-ai-a-hands-on-guide/)

Multi-Turn Reinforcement Learning (MTRL) on Amazon SageMaker AI trains models on the outcome of a whole multi-step task rather than labeled steps. This hands-on guide walks the full pipeline using Wordle: deploying the agent and reward on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, preparing the dataset, training, and evaluating at serving settings.
