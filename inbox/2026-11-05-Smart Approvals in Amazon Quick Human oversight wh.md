Smart Approvals in Amazon Quick reviews eligible agent actions within your existing controls, reducing unnecessary approval prompts while keeping consequential decisions with a person. See how policy sets the boundary, how a classifier reviews actions, and how AWS CloudTrail and Amazon CloudWatch Logs support audit.
