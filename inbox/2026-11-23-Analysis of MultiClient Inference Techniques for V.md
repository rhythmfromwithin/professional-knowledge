# Analysis of Multi-Client Inference Techniques for Vision-Language-Action Models
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26944](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26944)

arXiv:2611.26944v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) are a new class of machine learning model for generalizable robot control. While they enable robots to perform complex tasks in unseen environments, serving VLAs leaves hardware idle between inference passes and under-utilized during inference. Serving multiple clients at once can improve utilization, but fundamental multi-client techniques such as batching and co-execution have not been characterized for VLAs. We therefore characterize the trade-offs these known techniques impose across latency, throughput, power, and hardware utilization. Our findings inform trade-offs and challenges for future multi-VLA serving systems.
