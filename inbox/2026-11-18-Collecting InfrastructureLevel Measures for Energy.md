# Collecting Infrastructure-Level Measures for Energy Analysis in Modern Computing Infrastructures
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22160)

arXiv:2611.22160v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Modern HPC facilities are complex environments in which all components are tightly coupled; consequently, precisely modeling and accounting for energy consumption requires approaches that go beyond instrumenting individual compute nodes. Aiming for a cluster-wide vision, we designed a scalable data collection pipeline that aggregates fine-grained measurements from multiple subsystems-spanning component loads, software and hardware counters, sensors and energy-ensuring consistent sampling frequencies and synchronized data acquisition across the infrastructure. We present a dataset of comprehensive metrics collected at 1 Hz over one month from a production research data center supporting heterogeneous workloads, including HPC, Kubernetes services, and distributed storage. To illustrate what this approach makes possible in practice, we analyze two case studies-distributed I/O and large-language-model inference-showing how cluster-wide measurement reveals system-level dynamics missed by localized instrumentation. The proposed dataset and collection methodology provide a foundation for future work on predictive modeling, energy-aware resource management, and more efficient computing infrastructures.
