# Visualize points and routes with GeoJSON in Amazon Quick Sight layer maps
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/visualize-points-and-routes-with-geojson-in-amazon-quick-sight-layer-maps/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/visualize-points-and-routes-with-geojson-in-amazon-quick-sight-layer-maps/)

Amazon Quick Sight layer maps now support Point and LineString geometry from the same GeoJSON workflow, so you can plot store locations and the routes between them. This post covers the dataset and GeoJSON structure layer maps require, when to choose points or lines, and techniques for viewport control, layering geometries, and dynamic GeoJSON updates.
