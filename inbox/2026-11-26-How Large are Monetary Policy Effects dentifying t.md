# How Large are Monetary Policy Effects? dentifying the Shocks in a VAR also Identifies the Constants
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34722](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34722)

arXiv:2611.34722v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Restrictions on the contemporaneous effects matrix used to identify fundamental shocks in a structural VAR, also determine the mapping from the structural constant terms to the reduced form constant terms. In some models one will have priors about these structural constant terms and these should therefore be included in a Bayesian estimation procedure. We illustrate the potential significance of this using the analysis of monetary policy by Baumeister and Hamilton (2018) as a motivating example. We show that imposing priors over the structural constant terms can lead to a more intuitive estimated monetary policy rule and a much larger estimated role for monetary policy in describing the evolution of the data. The analysis also allows for a clear visual presentation of the structural model, highlighting its multiple contending elements. For example the observed inflation series is estimated to be the result of positive demand and commodity price effects offset by negative monetary policy and risk spread effects.
