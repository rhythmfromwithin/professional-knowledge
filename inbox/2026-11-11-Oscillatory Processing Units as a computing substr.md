# Oscillatory Processing Units as a computing substrate for biomimetic intelligence
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13840](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13840)

arXiv:2611.13840v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Biological intelligence emerges from neural activity that connects sensation, memory and action. Synaptic plasticity, neuronal assemblies and oscillations provide principles for biomimetic computing, but translating them into reusable operations requires a defined computational unit. We introduce Oscillatory Processing Units (OPUs), groups of synthetic neurons sharing a rhythm. Their directed interactions transform incoming activity and prior receiving state into an Oscillatory Occupancy Space (OOS), which becomes available to subsequent computation. We show that source--receiver conditions and oscillatory organization shape these transformations, including their geometry, persistence and historical accessibility. An Atlas of measured relationships supports prospective composition with predicted response classes. Configured circuits perform logical and numerical operations, express associative recall and generate closed-loop decisions with fixed synaptic weights. OPUs and OOS establish a computing substrate for biomimetic intelligence in which experience remains part of the state through which new input becomes action.
