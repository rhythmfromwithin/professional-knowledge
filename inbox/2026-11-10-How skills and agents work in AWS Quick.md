# How skills and agents work in AWS Quick
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-skills-and-agents-work-in-aws-quick/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-skills-and-agents-work-in-aws-quick/)

Repeatable tasks like status updates, release notes, and ticket creation can be encoded in AWS Quick through agents and skills. This post covers how agents, skills, and your data fit together in AWS Quick, how to build and share skills for your team, and how the official catalog and custom agents help you use them.
