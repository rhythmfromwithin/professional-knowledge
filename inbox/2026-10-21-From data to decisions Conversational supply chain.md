# From data to decisions: Conversational supply chain intelligence with Amazon Quick and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/from-data-to-decisions-conversational-supply-chain-intelligence-with-amazon-quick-and-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/from-data-to-decisions-conversational-supply-chain-intelligence-with-amazon-quick-and-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)

Enterprise supply chains are data-rich but insight-poor, with the data to decide scattered across ERP, monitoring, procurement, and email. This post shows how Amazon Bedrock AgentCore exposes those systems as a governed tool layer, and how Amazon Quick turns them into one conversational experience for business users, with a deploy-it-yourself walkthrough.
