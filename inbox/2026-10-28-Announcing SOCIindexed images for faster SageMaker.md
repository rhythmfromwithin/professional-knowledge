# Announcing SOCI-indexed images for faster SageMaker AI jobs
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/announcing-soci-indexed-images-for-faster-sagemaker-ai-jobs/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/announcing-soci-indexed-images-for-faster-sagemaker-ai-jobs/)

Seekable OCI (SOCI) indexing for Amazon SageMaker AI training and processing jobs takes full container image download off the critical path. SageMaker AI lazy-loads your image to start containers in seconds instead of minutes, with no changes to your job definition. See the benchmarks and how to adopt it.
