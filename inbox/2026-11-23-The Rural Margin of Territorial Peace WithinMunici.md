# The Rural Margin of Territorial Peace: Within-Municipality Evidence from Colombia
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26882](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26882)

arXiv:2611.26882v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Colombia's peace agreement introduced participatory development programs and congressional constituencies reserved for conflict victims with a rural electorate. I use repeated waves of the Social Capital Barometer and a triple-difference event study to compare changes in rural-urban engagement gaps in municipalities covered by both reforms with those elsewhere. Rural engagement evolved more favorably relative to urban engagement in the covered municipalities. The index captures coordination around shared problems, civic participation and party linkage, with coordination providing the clearest evidence. The result persists when the reference period precedes the agreement and the baseline wave is excluded. Program activity and organizational membership patterns are consistent with participatory investment and political mobilization. The findings document a change in engagement beyond voting under the joint territorial reforms.
