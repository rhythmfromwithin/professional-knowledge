# Auditing Hyperstimulation at Scale in Child-Facing YouTube Videos
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21637)

arXiv:2611.21637v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Parents, pediatricians, and journalists have raised concern that child-facing videos are becoming increasingly overstimulating, but this concern rests mostly on ad hoc observation rather than systematic measurement. We present a large-scale audit of hyperstimulation patterns, which we refer to as content-independent production choices that may exceed young children's capacity to process comfortably. Drawing on parents' Reddit discussions and developmental psychology, we identify 12 hyperstimulation patterns and operationalize them as computational metrics. We apply these metrics to 16,375 videos from 542 child-facing YouTube channels spanning 2015-2026. We found broad increases in stimulation over time, with cut rates rising across all five content categories. Nearly a quarter of the channels increased in at least one stimulation pattern over time. More stimulating videos also tended to receive more views, but the relevant stimulation patterns differed across content types.
