# A Matter of Representation: Evolving Partial Spreads for Bent Functions
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26011)

arXiv:2611.26011v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Partial spread ($\mathcal{PS}$) bent functions are Boolean functions of maximum nonlinearity built from families of subspaces that pairwise intersect only in the null vector. We design an Evolution Strategy (ES) to construct such partial spread families, comparing two encodings: one based on generator matrices of the subspaces, and one based on spread sets. With the latter, combined with a ruin-and-rebuild operator, the ES finds $\mathcal{PS}$ functions up to $n=10$ variables. An ablation study shows that mutation is necessary, while the population size is not, and that a restarted look-ahead greedy heuristic outperforms the ES on the hardest instance. We investigate this finding with a landscape analysis of the stagnation points, and we also introduce a quality-diversity variant of the ES which finds $\mathcal{PS}$ functions of low algebraic degree for 8 variables. Interestingly, the bent functions evolved by the ES have higher 2-ranks and mostly higher degrees than those obtained by a baseline Genetic Programming optimizer.
