# Combinatorial Characterizations of the Learnability of Conditional Distributions
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31890](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31890)

arXiv:2610.31890v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: We study the PAC-learnability of function classes that map inputs to probability distributions over a finite space. The learner observes i.i.d. input-label pairs, with each label sampled from the conditional distribution of an unknown target hypothesis in the class. We measure learning through excess risk under a bounded proper loss that satisfies two mild assumptions. We introduce the \textbf{fat$\_\gamma$Natarajan dimension}, which generalizes both fat$\_\gamma$ and Natarajan dimension, and prove that its finiteness at every positive scale is necessary and sufficient for learnability. We also introduce a \textbf{fat$\_\gamma$Graph} dimension extending the classical \gra dimension and establish inequalities comparing the two dimensions at different scales, leading to an equivalent characterization of learnability. Finally, we show that a distribution-valued function class is learnable if and only if each of its coordinate classes is learnable in the probabilistic concept model.
