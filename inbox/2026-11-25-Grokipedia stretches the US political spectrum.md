# Grokipedia stretches the US political spectrum
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32420)

arXiv:2611.32420v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Language models now write reference works that people and other AI systems consult. Grokipedia, written by xAI's model, presents itself as a less biased alternative to crowd-edited Wikipedia. We compare the biographies of 1,614 Democratic and Republican current and former politicians, from Andrew Jackson to members of the current Congress, on both encyclopedias in February and November 2026. For nine in ten Republicans, Grokipedia's biography reads as if written by a more conservative author than Wikipedia's. For Democrats, this holds only about half the time. We find the politicians' ideology explains this asymmetry. For 1,022 politicians with Congressional roll call voting records, how conservative the author seems rises linearly with the politician's ideology and about twice as steeply on Grokipedia as on Wikipedia. Republicans in races where Elon Musk's America PAC spent money show no extra bias. Rather than favoring a party, Grokipedia stretches the political spectrum, moving the biographies of the most liberal politicians further left and of the most conservative politicians further right.
