# FittedGenes: Domain-Aware Sewing Pattern Layout Optimization
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28022)

arXiv:2611.28022v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Two-dimensional cutting and packing problems aim to arrange pieces on a larger surface without overlap while minimizing material waste. Although extensively studied in industries such as textiles, traditional nesting research assumes piece geometries are fixed. In this work, we make the garment design itself a variable of the nesting optimization: we introduce a new formulation of the sewing pattern nesting problem in which panel geometry is modified under domain-aware constraints, preserving fit while allowing appearance to vary within designer-set bounds. We present an evolutionary nesting framework that embeds these modifications directly into a genetic algorithm (GA). Two novel mutation operators are proposed: one enables splitting panels into stitchable sub-panels, and the other adjusts garment design while preserving fit. Our method is evaluated on existing irregular nesting benchmarks and a new benchmark derived from a comprehensive dataset of sewing patterns. Our experiments demonstrate that evolutionary algorithms enhanced with the proposed garment-aware mutations achieve markedly better results than versions restricted to standard permutation-based operators, demonstrating the value of integrating design flexibility into nesting optimization. Our implementation is available at https://github.com/fittedgenes/GarmentCode-Nesting.
