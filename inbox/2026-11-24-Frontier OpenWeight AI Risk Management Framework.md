# Frontier Open-Weight AI Risk Management Framework
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28082)

arXiv:2611.28082v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Open-weight models advance transparency, research, and innovation, and spread the benefits of AI more widely. They also carry distinct risks. A release cannot be undone, safeguards can be removed through fine-tuning, and no single party can monitor use or withdraw the model. Managing these risks therefore requires an approach tailored to open release. This framework addresses the governance challenge at the heart of that choice. Co-developed by Concordia AI and Z.AI, it adapts the full risk management lifecycle, from risk assessment to mitigation and governance, to the unique properties of open-weight models. This document does not resolve every tension between openness and safety. Rather, it provides the first comprehensive, evidence-based foundation for navigating these tensions. As frontier open-weight releases accelerate, risk management best practice and standards are urgently needed. We invite researchers, developers, policymakers, and civil society to build on this framework and advance the science and practice of open-weight AI governance.
