# Semantic diffing of formal policies with anti-unification
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/semantic-diffing-of-formal-policies-with-anti-unification/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/semantic-diffing-of-formal-policies-with-anti-unification/)

A textual diff of two formal policies flags every cosmetic change and hides the one that alters behavior. This post shows how to compute a symbolic, deterministic semantic diff with an open-source anti-unification engine, so reviewers see only the genuine disagreements between two candidate authorization policies.
