# Announcing new ways to customize models with SageMaker AI, without managing GPUs
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/announcing-new-ways-to-customize-models-with-sagemaker-ai-without-managing-gpus/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/announcing-new-ways-to-customize-models-with-sagemaker-ai-without-managing-gpus/)

Amazon SageMaker AI serverless model customization now includes training sessions, generally available today. You write and coordinate your own post-training loop (custom loss functions, reward signals, or multi-turn reinforcement learning) while SageMaker AI handles GPU allocation, distributed execution, fault recovery, and checkpoint storage.
