# How Natera designed an agentic AI system for lab ops with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-natera-designed-an-agentic-ai-system-for-lab-ops-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-natera-designed-an-agentic-ai-system-for-lab-ops-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)

Natera built SWARM, an agentic AI system on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore that captures institutional lab-operations knowledge and deploys it at scale. This post walks through the full architecture, from knowledge base construction to deployment and evaluation, delivering about 130x faster first response and 96.2% automated coverage across support tickets.
