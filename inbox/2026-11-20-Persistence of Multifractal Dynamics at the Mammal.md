# Persistence of Multifractal Dynamics at the Mammalian Neuromuscular Junction Across Extracellular Calcium Conditions
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25139)

arXiv:2611.25139v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: The neuromuscular junction represents a relevant experimental model for revealing important biophysical mechanisms underlying synaptic transmission. In this work, we revisited the time-series analysis of electrophysiological recordings obtained from mammalian diaphragm neuromuscular junctions. We applied multifractal detrended fluctuation analysis (MFDFA) to assess whether intervals between spontaneous potentials exhibit multifractal behavior at physiological extracellular calcium concentrations and at hypocalcemic and hypercalcemic levels. Surprisingly, we observed a robust, stable multifractal profile across all electrophysiological recordings, regardless of calcium concentration. Therefore, our results provide support for a rich and complex scenario governing neurotransmitter release. Based on these findings, we discuss their biophysical significance and physiological implications.
