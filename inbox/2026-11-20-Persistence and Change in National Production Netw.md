# Persistence and Change in National Production Networks
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25869)

arXiv:2611.25869v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Many country-years in multi-country input-output databases have no national table. We study the kernel of domestic intermediate transactions, the part that rescaling to new industry totals cannot update. Among 25 economies with a national table every year, the latest table misplaces 6.6 percent of the kernel one year ahead and 21.2 percent ten years ahead, while the five most similar economies misplace 24.1 to 25.0 percent at every horizon. In the OECD tables, 88 percent of country-years without a national table lie close to one cross-country template, against 3.6 percent of country-years with a table. The template spreads from 29 economies in 2019 to 67 in 2022. In this sample an own table's error meets its distance to that year's template near fifteen years, not as a general shelf life.
