# How Amazon Marketplace evaluates content quality with Amazon SageMaker AI
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-amazon-marketplace-evaluates-content-quality-with-amazon-sagemaker-ai/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-amazon-marketplace-evaluates-content-quality-with-amazon-sagemaker-ai/)

Learn how Amazon Marketplace evaluates A+ Content quality at scale on Amazon SageMaker AI by rendering each product page as a single snapshot, scoring it with a vision-language model fused with OCR readability signals in a CatBoost classifier, and using SHAP to turn each decision into prioritized, actionable recommendations for selling partners.
