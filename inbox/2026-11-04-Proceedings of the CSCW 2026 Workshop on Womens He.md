# Proceedings of the CSCW 2026 Workshop on Women's Health as Cooperative Work: Participatory AI Across Care, Community, and Policy
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03776)

arXiv:2611.03776v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: This submission indexes contributions to the CSCW 2026 workshop, "Women's Health as Cooperative Work: Participatory AI Across Care, Community, and Policy," held on October 11, 2026. The workshop examines women's health as cooperative work spanning patients, caregivers, clinicians, communities, institutions, and digital technologies. Contributions explore participatory AI, coordination breakdowns, the distribution of care labor, governance, decision-making authority, and accountability across care, community, and policy contexts.
The index provides paper titles, authors, affiliations, thematic areas, and unique report numbers to support discovery of the corresponding individual arXiv submissions.
