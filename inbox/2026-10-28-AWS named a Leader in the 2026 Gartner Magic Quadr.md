# AWS named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service

AWS has been named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) for AWS Connect Customer. This recognition marks an important milestone for our team, and it reflects years of work pushing on what's possible in customer experience.
