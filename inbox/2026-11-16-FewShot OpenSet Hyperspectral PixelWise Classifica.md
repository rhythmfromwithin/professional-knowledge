# Few-Shot Open-Set Hyperspectral Pixel-Wise Classification with Spatial Aware Label Propagation
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17974)

arXiv:2611.17974v1 Announce Type: cross
Abstract: Pixelwise classification of hyperspectral images (HSI) is a fundamental task in remote sensing, but its deployment in real-world applications is often limited by the scarcity of labeled data and the presence of unknown classes at inference time. In this work, we propose Few-shot Open-set hyperspectral recognition from Self-Supervised Learning training (FOSSeL), a unified framework for few-shot, open-set hyperspectral pixelwise classification that does not require supervised training. The proposed approach first leverages self-supervised learning to construct a discriminative feature space from unlabeled data. Then, given a small set of labeled seed pixels, class predictions are obtained through spatially-aware graph-based label propagation over superpixel representations, enabling the joint exploitation of spectral similarity and spatial consistency. To address the open-set scenario, we introduce simple yet effective strategies to identify pixels that do not belong to the known classes. We evaluate FOSSeL on three benchmark hyperspectral datasets under multiple few-shot settings. The results show that the proposed framework achieves competitive or superior performance compared to fully supervised methods while maintaining robustness in the presence of unknown classes. These findings highlight the potential of combining self-supervised feature learning with graph-based inference for practical HSI analysis in low-annotation and open-world scenarios.
