# Full Fine-Tuning (FFT) on SageMaker AI Serverless Model Customization

Amazon SageMaker AI Serverless Model Customization now supports Full Fine-Tuning (FFT). This post explores when FFT outperforms LoRA across key benchmarks, examines the spectral properties of model weight matrices behind the quality gap, shares practical hyperparameter tuning guidelines, and shows how to get started.
