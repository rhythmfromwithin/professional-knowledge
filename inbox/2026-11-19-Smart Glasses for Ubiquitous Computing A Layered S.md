# Smart Glasses for Ubiquitous Computing: A Layered Survey and the Road Ahead
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23534)

arXiv:2611.23534v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: We conduct a systematic survey of smart-glasses research for ubiquitous computing. Our search covers work published from 2020 to October 2026, with selected earlier work included for historical context. After screening and title-level deduplication, we identify 163 smart-glasses papers and separately compile related resources, including 14 representative hardware platforms, their associated software tools and pretrained models, and 10 datasets. We organize this literature through four complementary layers. Research Resources covers hardware platforms, software tools and models, and datasets that support development and evaluation. Sensing & Perception examines how glasses measure and interpret information about users and their surroundings. Interaction & Assistance addresses how users express intentions and receive feedback and assistance. Applications situates these contributions in tasks and use contexts. Energy, latency, wearability, reliability, privacy, security, and social acceptability are treated as deployment constraints across all four layers. This framework provides a unified view of current research and a vision for how advances across the four layers could come together to support smart glasses as a ubiquitous computing platform. Building on this synthesis, we outline research directions for extending capabilities across tasks and over time, supporting assistance under human control, and achieving reliable, efficient, and sustained everyday use.
