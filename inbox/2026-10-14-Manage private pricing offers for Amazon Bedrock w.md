# Manage private pricing offers for Amazon Bedrock with managed entitlements
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/manage-private-pricing-offers-for-amazon-bedrock-with-managed-entitlements/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/manage-private-pricing-offers-for-amazon-bedrock-with-managed-entitlements/)

Managing AI model access across dozens or hundreds of AWS accounts creates friction when each account must accept AWS Marketplace Private Pricing Offers individually. This post shows two ways to activate MPPO entitlements in AWS License Manager: a lightweight script for a one-time fix, and CDK automation for ongoing event-driven processing.
