# Taxonomy of multi-agent design patterns, anti-patterns, and lessons from manufacturing
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/taxonomy-of-multi-agent-design-patterns-anti-patterns-and-lessons-from-manufacturing/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/taxonomy-of-multi-agent-design-patterns-anti-patterns-and-lessons-from-manufacturing/)

Multi-agent coordination is a distributed systems problem, not a model problem. This post gives a field-tested taxonomy of multi-agent design patterns and anti-patterns, a framework for deciding when multiple agents are warranted, and how to build cost-efficient, reliable systems on AWS.
