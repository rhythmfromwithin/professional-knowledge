# The Effect of Remote Working on Regional Waste: Evidence from South Korea
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31860)

arXiv:2611.31860v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Remote working, or work-from-home (WFH), has become common around the world. However, its broader effects on social welfare, including waste generation, remain understudied. This study examines the impact of remote work on regional waste production in South Korea, using the COVID-19 pandemic as a natural experiment. Overall household waste changed little, but regions with greater WFH eligibility saw increases in plastic and textile waste and a decrease in food waste. These findings suggest that remote work may change consumption patterns and daily routines. Understanding these effects can help inform sustainable work practices and waste management as remote work becomes more widespread.
