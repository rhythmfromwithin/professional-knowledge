# Which Archived Results Survive a Rule Change? A Case Study in Revalidating Decisions
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26666)

arXiv:2611.26666v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: A research workflow can retain a correct numerical result after the evidence or approval that supported its use has expired. Common incremental build methods identify reusable computations, while deciding what remains usable also requires knowing which policy justified each approval. We examined a recorded parser migration across 312 paired research records, replayed both parsers with the same inputs, applied a calculation-file omission to the complete cohort, and evaluated current proof in a GNU Make comparison. Treating the prior projections as current would change at least one field in 11 of 312 records, and controlled cases show that even matching values can lack a current proof. The study separates numerical recovery from applicable approval and sets out the conditions under which a maintained dependency graph can preserve both.
