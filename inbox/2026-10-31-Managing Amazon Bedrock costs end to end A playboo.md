# Managing Amazon Bedrock costs end to end: A playbook for FinOps and platform teams
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/managing-amazon-bedrock-costs-end-to-end-a-playbook-for-finops-and-platform-teams/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/managing-amazon-bedrock-costs-end-to-end-a-playbook-for-finops-and-platform-teams/)

This post presents a five-stage operating model for managing Amazon Bedrock cost: attribute every dollar to an owner, make every request observable, enforce budgets, optimize platform defaults, and operate a review cadence. Written for FinOps and platform engineering teams.
