# Amazon Polly delivers a major upgrade to its Generative engine
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/amazon-polly-delivers-a-major-upgrade-to-its-generative-engine/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/amazon-polly-delivers-a-major-upgrade-to-its-generative-engine/)

The Amazon Polly Generative engine gets a major upgrade: more expressive, more accurate speech with low latency, plus 14 new voices across 9 locales and general availability of Hindi and Finnish. This drop-in upgrade requires no code changes and is now generally available in production.
