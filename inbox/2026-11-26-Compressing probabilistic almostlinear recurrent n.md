# Compressing probabilistic almost-linear recurrent networks without refitting
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34203](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34203)

arXiv:2611.34203v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Can a compressed recurrent network reuse its learned noise model? We applied finite-horizon balanced truncation to ten probabilistic almost-linear networks on delayed recall. Reducing 64 states to 32 without refitting met forecast tolerances for seven networks when balancing included rectifier inputs, compared with one using the readout alone. Including rectifier inputs improved agreement with the parent's mean-centred forecast distribution on all ten networks, with a median reduction in discrepancy of 65%. Compared with random directions, rectifier inputs reduced centred divergence on six networks and mean forecast error on all ten. Both full and reduced networks underestimated uncertainty on weak cues.
