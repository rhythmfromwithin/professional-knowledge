# Developers can now access SageMaker Studio through the new AWS experience
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/developers-can-now-access-sagemaker-studio-through-the-new-aws-experience/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/developers-can-now-access-sagemaker-studio-through-the-new-aws-experience/)

New Amazon SageMaker AI customers can now land in a fully configured SageMaker Studio environment in only a few steps. Sign up with an identity you already have, such as Amazon, Apple, GitHub, or Google, with no advanced AWS account or credit card required for most customers, plus $100 in AWS Free Tier credits.
