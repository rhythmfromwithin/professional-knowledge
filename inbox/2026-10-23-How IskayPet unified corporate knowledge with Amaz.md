# How IskayPet unified corporate knowledge with Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Bases
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-iskaypet-unified-corporate-knowledge-with-amazon-bedrock-managed-knowledge-bases/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-iskaypet-unified-corporate-knowledge-with-amazon-bedrock-managed-knowledge-bases/)

Learn how IskayPet, a leading pet care company in Iberia, built a multilingual corporate knowledge assistant on Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Bases. The assistant answers employee questions across retail, veterinary, onboarding, and procurement in four languages and three countries, with no RAG infrastructure to manage.
