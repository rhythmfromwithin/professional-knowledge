# Risk-optimal active learning with informative abstention
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24986](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24986)

arXiv:2611.24986v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: We study label acquisition when an oracle may abstain and its response probability shares parameters with the classifier. A query can then provide information through both a returned label and the response outcome. Under a correctly specified parametric model, we derive the classifier information after adjustment for response-model nuisance parameters. Combining this information with the local curvature of multiclass zero-one risk gives an acquisition criterion for minimizing the leading asymptotic excess risk. We characterize the optimal allocation under a fixed query budget and show how query allocation affects response identifiability. The oracle criterion reduces to response-discounted acquisition when response probability does not depend on the classifier parameters. Simulations examine the resulting query allocations and isolate the contribution of the response-outcome term through an ablation. A Consumer Complaints replay retains observed human abstentions and reveals benchmark labels upon response. Response-aware acquisition has lower mean test error than response-discounted acquisition at the two largest budgets, with paired differences significant after Holm correction. Entropy sampling performs better at the smallest budget. The gains depend on the response mechanism and query budget.
