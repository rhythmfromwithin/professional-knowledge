# DOCKET: A Pluralistic Benchmark for Evaluating AI-Assisted Policy Feedback in U.S. Rulemaking
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17914](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17914)

arXiv:2611.17914v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: As AI models advance and impact deliberative democracies, there is a growing need to assess their support of pluralistic viewpoints. Most pluralism benchmarks are insufficient for this: they are not grounded in diversity (i.e., stakeholders across different frames, skills and needs), quality (i.e., rigorous and technical debates), and impact (i.e., invested deliberators and real-world outcomes). To address this, we first (i) curate DOCKET, the most comprehensive dataset of the U.S. Federal Notice & Comment process to date. DOCKET consists of 42.3M public comments on 22,400 regulations across 44 government agencies between 2016 and 2026 and contains millions of policy debates; we link public comments to particular policy items proposed by regulators to track the public's attention and the responsiveness of regulators to public input. (ii) Next, we demonstrate the pluralistic utility of DOCKET - we show that public commenters have a wide breadth of perspectives with significant depth in policy expertise and that regulators are often responsive. (iii) Finally, we present DOCKETBench, the first systematic evaluation of LLMs' ability to represent human policy perspectives in federal rulemaking. Only 18-30% of LLM-generated feedback aligns with real human comments. LLMs critique proposals broadly but generally, lacking the specific grounding, citations, and lived-experience arguments that humans bring to bear; in addition, most models we test systematically agree with government perspectives (overlapping with up to 68% of their claims) and agree least with labor unions (as little as 26% of their claims), demonstrating a fundamental skew in perspectives that even tools like persona modeling do not solve. Taken together, we reveal fundamental gaps in how language models navigate real policy issues, and DOCKETBench serves as a testbed for closing those gaps in a crucial real-world domain.
