# Taste or Strategy? Nationalistic Bias and Competitor Penalties in Figure Skating Judging
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13194)

arXiv:2611.13194v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Many decisions rest on expert panels whose members have ties to some candidates, and favoritism is hard to tell from shared taste or better information. Using a rich dataset from figure skating, we find evidence of a strategic compatriot premium and a targeted penalty on the compatriot's close rivals. The premium falls along a randomly drawn start order and, before 2022/23, rises when close rivals have their own judges on the panel. The penalty grows with the compatriot's placement chances and fades once she is out of contention. A counterfactual analysis shows that these biases can shape close competitions.
