# A General Framework for E-process Stopping Rules in Best-Arm Identification
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31898)

arXiv:2611.31898v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: We propose an e-process stopping rule in the best-arm identification problem. The e-process stopping rule is different from the Chernoff stopping rule by its hypothesis structure on the stopping decision. The major benefit of an e-process stopping rule is the flexibility in stopping rule design and in fact, it recovers previously suggested Chernoff stopping rule on several bandit problems. Moreover, we analyze the asymptotic optimality of the $\delta$-PAC algorithm with the decomposition of e-processes. The decomposition separates optimality conditions into three parts; the sampling policy allocation convergence, the efficiency of e-process design, and the bandit problem's inherent stochastic behavior. Subsequently, we prove a sufficient condition for asymptotic optimality in bandit algorithms. For the application of an e-process stopping rule, we derived an asymptotically optimal algorithm for the generalized linear bandit problem.
