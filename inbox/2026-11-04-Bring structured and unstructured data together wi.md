# Bring structured and unstructured data together with Amazon Quick

Amazon Quick brings structured and unstructured data together in one experience. In this post, you connect Amazon S3 as a knowledge base, enrich a dataset, build a Space, and create a custom chat agent that answers business questions across both data types through a single conversational interface.
