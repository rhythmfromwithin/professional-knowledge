# Faculty Experience with Generative AI Integration into the Teaching and Learning Process
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27998)

arXiv:2611.27998v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: This study was conducted to enhance our knowledge and understanding of the lived experiences of faculty regarding generative artificial intelligence (AI) in teaching and learning within the English and Communication Program at the American University of Armenia. This phenomenological qualitative research study used face-to-face, in-depth, open-ended interviews with five faculty members from the program. Faculty expressed different levels of conflict, voicing both positive and negative issues and concerns regarding their experiences with generative AI in the teaching and learning process. Five categories and 11 themes emerged from the analysis of these interviews, including concerns about academic integrity and student learning, as well as a lack of faculty AI training, understanding, and experience. Emergent themes indicated that faculty need additional support, including training and leadership guidance, in order to better understand the importance and benefits of effective AI utilization and AI literacy development. Faculty lacked sufficient experience with and understanding of generative AI capabilities and ongoing developments. Overall, faculty perceptions and attitudes toward generative AI within teaching and learning can be characterized as a mix of reservation and optimism.
