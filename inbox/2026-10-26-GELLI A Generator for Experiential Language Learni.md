# GELLI: A Generator for Experiential Language Learning Interactions
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31987)

arXiv:2610.31987v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Learning a second language requires extensive practice across situations personal to each learner, yet the most effective existing approaches --- narrative situations designed around specific learning goals and delivered through media --- demand substantial authoring effort, limiting practice to the scope of what was built. We present GELLI, a system that generates narrative practice worlds from a learner's goals: situated scenes with characters to converse with, support alongside the interaction, and reflection, all produced at runtime with no pre authored content. In a within-subjects study with 40 learners of Japanese, GELLI produced greater vocabulary recall and confidence than expert-authored videos targeting the same goals, with no significant difference in speaking performance. Learners turned remembered or imagined situations into experiences they could shape and learn through. Support made experimentation feel safe, while unexpected narrative developments and language suggestions gave them new possibilities to pursue beyond what they initially intended to practice.
