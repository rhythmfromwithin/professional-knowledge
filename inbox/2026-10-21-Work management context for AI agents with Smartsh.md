# Work management context for AI agents with Smartsheet MCP on AWS
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/work-management-context-for-ai-agents-with-smartsheet-mcp-on-aws/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/work-management-context-for-ai-agents-with-smartsheet-mcp-on-aws/)

The Smartsheet Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server lets AI agents read, update, and act on live work management data. This post shows two ways to connect it on AWS: Amazon Quick for business teams and Kiro for developers, so your agents can work with Smartsheet without switching tools.
