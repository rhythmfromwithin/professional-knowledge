# Four Amazon Bedrock inference choices that affect cost
> 原文: [https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/four-amazon-bedrock-inference-choices-that-affect-cost/](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/four-amazon-bedrock-inference-choices-that-affect-cost/)

Your Amazon Bedrock inference bill can keep climbing even after you tune prompts and application logic. Four request-level choices (cache duration, service tier, reasoning effort, and maxTokens) change how many tokens the service processes and the rate applied. We show how we tested each and how to verify the effect.
