# Symbolic Proof Compression and Fractal Universality: A Conditional Non-Constructive Refutation of the Riemann Hypothesis
> 原文: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31469](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31469)

arXiv:2611.31469v1 Announce Type: new
Abstract: Building on S.~C.~Woon's (1994) fractal and symbolic reformulation of Voronin's Universality Theorem, we construct a formal, non-constructive proof-by-contradiction architecture exploring the Riemann Hypothesis ($\mathsf{RH}$). Woon's Corollary~3 permits arbitrary finite binary strings to be encoded into nowhere-vanishing exponential waveforms on a closed disk inside the critical strip. Combining effective upper bounds for Voronin universality (Good, Garunk\v{s}tis) with Cauchy-integral Taylor decoding yields an explicit finite rational grid $\mathcal{G}\_{L\_0}$ below an effective ceiling $H\_{\mathrm{crit}}(L\_0)$ within which every formal proof of bit-length at most $L\_0$ is imprinted with zero flipped digits. We introduce a conjectural \emph{Zeta-Universal Symbolic Proof Compression Hypothesis} ($\mathsf{SPC}\_{\mathsf{RH}}$), positing that the global regularity implied by $\mathsf{RH}$ compresses true $\Pi\_1^0$ statements about $\zeta(s)$ into $\mathsf{ZFC}$ derivations of bounded length $L\_0$. Assuming classical logic and $\mathsf{SPC}\_{\mathsf{RH}}$, G\"odel--Carnap diagonalization over bounded zeta-encoded provability yields a contradiction. The self-referential result converts a digital encoding framework with any uniform proof-compression principle for RH into an immediate non-constructive refutation of RH. Given that, with human input and computer assistance, $\mathsf{RH}$ has been formally verified in the Lean theorem prover strictly within constructive logic, this contradiction does not refute $\mathsf{RH}$. Instead, it falsifies $\mathsf{SPC}\_{\mathsf{RH}}$. We conclude by isolating the exact proof-theoretic obstruction---Pudl\'ak's lower bound on bounded reflection proofs---showing why the analytic regularity of $\mathsf{RH}$ cannot compress logical self-reference, and exposing the limits of applying the Law of Excluded Middle to uncomputably bounded searches.
